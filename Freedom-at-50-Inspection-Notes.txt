Freedom at 50 — Inspection Notes
(Nothing was changed)

Freedom at 50 is a personal finance progress app you can add to your iPhone Home Screen. Your journeys, goals, and pension figures live only on that phone (in Safari’s local storage), not in the cloud.

--------------------------------------------------
1. Most important problems right now
--------------------------------------------------

1. Several Save buttons do not work
   On Goals, Save for House Fund, Holiday Fund, and Personal Savings, and on Pension, Save Pension, call a missing step in the app. Your typed numbers are not written into the phone’s saved copy.
   What still works: editing journeys (when you leave a field), adding journeys/goals, House Pocket “Record Payment,” and custom goals when you change a field and leave it.

2. Delete buttons do nothing
   Delete appears on goals, but tapping it has no effect.

3. “Restore original starting journeys” can wipe your real journeys with one tap
   It replaces your current journeys with the built-in starter list, with no “Are you sure?” warning.

4. No real Backup
   More → Backup & export is wording only. If Safari clears site data, or the app is removed in a way that clears storage, your figures can be gone with no easy restore.

5. The Home screen is duplicated
   Two nearly identical Home menus were left in the file (different version labels). On iPhone this can make menus/buttons feel odd, double-fire, or unreliable.

6. Bottom “Home” is unreliable
   The floating menu button is the dependable way home; the bottom Home tab can fail to show the Home wallpaper menu.

7. Offline / update helper is half-finished
   The background updater was set to “always try the live site” and clear old caches, but it no longer keeps a proper offline copy. Offline use is weak; update banners can feel confusing.

8. Half-built More items
   Demo Mode, Companion, Reports, and Backup are placeholders or unreachable. Not urgent for daily use, but they look unfinished.

9. App file is very heavy
   The Home wallpaper is embedded twice inside the page, so the main file is huge (~490KB of HTML alone, plus large Overview/Journey images). First open on mobile data can feel slow.

--------------------------------------------------
2. What to fix first, and why
--------------------------------------------------

Priority 1 — Make Goals/Pension Save work
Why: You’re editing real money figures that silently don’t stick.

Priority 2 — Add a confirm (or safer wording) before restoring starter journeys
Why: One accidental tap can replace your journeys.

Priority 3 — Make Delete work, or remove the button
Why: A dead Delete button invites confusion and false confidence.

Priority 4 — Clean up the duplicate Home menu
Why: Daily navigation on iPhone should feel single and predictable.

Priority 5 — Add a simple Backup / Export
Why: Protects everything else if the phone clears app data.

Priority 6 — Fix offline/update behaviour carefully
Why: Improves reliability without redesign.

Priority 7 — Polish later (Demo, Companion, Reports, speed, small UI bugs)
Why: Nice to have after the app is trustworthy.

--------------------------------------------------
3. What you’d notice on your iPhone after each fix
--------------------------------------------------

1. Save fixed
   Change House Fund / Holiday / Savings / Pension → tap Save → see “Saved locally ✓” and the number still there after closing and reopening the app.

2. Restore warning
   Restore starter journeys asks for confirmation; Cancel leaves your journeys alone.

3. Delete fixed or removed
   Delete either removes/hides a goal as intended, or the button disappears so you aren’t misled.

4. Home cleaned up
   One Home menu; buttons respond once; version label consistent.

5. Backup
   A way to copy/export your figures (and later restore them).

6. Offline/update
   Opens more reliably without network; updates feel clearer, less “stuck on old version.”

7. Polish
   Demo/Companion/Reports become real or stop looking unfinished; app may open faster.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Risks to your existing data and design
--------------------------------------------------

Saved journeys, goals, pension
- Low risk if we change things carefully and keep the same storage name (freedom50-mobile-v1)
- Higher risk if we rename that storage or change how data is loaded — the app could “forget” your figures

Wallpapers / look
- Low risk if we don’t touch wallpaper files or redesign screens
- Higher risk if we replace images or restyle

House Pocket / progress math
- Low risk for Save/Home/Delete fixes
- Higher risk if we change how House Pocket links to House Fund — totals you see could change

Design redesign
- Not needed for the first fixes
- Big visual rewrites should wait for your approval

Already risky in the app today (before any new work):
- The restore-journeys button
- Having no backup

A careful first pass can fix Save, confirmations, Delete, and Home without changing wallpapers or wiping data — as long as we keep the same storage name and data shape, and you approve before anything ships.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Recommended order of work
--------------------------------------------------

1. Fix Save for House Fund, Holiday Fund, Personal Savings, and Pension
2. Add confirmation before restoring original journeys
3. Fix or remove Delete
4. Remove the duplicate Home and make Home navigation reliable
5. Add Backup / Export (and ideally Restore)
6. Stabilise updates / offline without changing your look
7. Finish or tidy More placeholders (Demo, Companion, Reports)
8. Optional speed/cleanup (duplicate wallpaper weight, small Overview quirks)

--------------------------------------------------
Bottom line
--------------------------------------------------

The app is usable for journeys and House Pocket, but Goals/Pension Save is broken, Delete is fake, journey restore is dangerous, and there’s no real backup. Fix trust and safety first; leave design and new features until you say go.
